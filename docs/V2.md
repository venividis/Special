# INTACT V2 — the plan

V2 turns the one-collection console into four things: a living, beautiful
NFT viewer that sells; a vault where coins are locked, bundled, gifted and
released slowly; a journal of memories written into each token; and a
studio where anyone launches a collection whose every token is a website
with its own room. The coin launchpad goes. This file is the
specification for V2 the way `DESIGN.md` and `BUILD-PLAN.md` are for V1.
Nothing here is built yet except the interface prototype
(`design/v2/prototype.html`). Every number below is a **budget** unless it
says **measured**.

## 1. What V2 stands on (surveyed 2026-10-19, file:line in the survey's report)

- **Locks** (`src/Locks.sol`) already holds ERC-20s and ETH for any
  beneficiary with `start`, `cliff`, `end` and a linear release; nobody can
  unlock early; `extend` only lengthens; `give` passes a lock to a
  canonical Reach. It holds one asset per lock, nothing sells on release,
  and its only console UI lives in the launch panel. The hub's fingerprint
  hashes `Locks.commitmentOf(account(id))`.
- **Pool** swaps with `to`, `minOut` and a deadline, and refuses any single
  trade above 50 % of the outgoing reserve. There is no oracle and no TWAP,
  so a drip has to protect itself (§3.1).
- **Reach** `executeBatch` runs up to 16 calls as the holder, so "swap,
  approve, lock, inscribe" is one transaction today. Sessions cannot batch.
- **Parley** keys rooms by the single hub's token ids (commons, groups,
  pairs, home rooms). Bodies live in events only (≤ 1,024 B plain,
  ≤ 4,096 B sealed). Sealing is client-side P-256. There are no per-collection
  rooms, and a home room cannot be made token-only.
- **The hub** is one collection with flat-price mints and two ERC-6551
  accounts per token. **Market** is INTACT-only.
- **The console** is at its ceilings: shell 14,955 of 15,000 B gzip, five of
  six panels within 26 B of 8,192. `Engine.PANELS = 6` is a constant, and
  Premises and the Catalog also assume six.

## 2. The experience: living light

The prototype (`design/v2/prototype.html`, one file, no host, no font, no
library) is the direction. What it establishes:

- **The soul.** A full-screen WebGL fragment shader: domain-warped fBm
  aurora, its palette the crest's own hues (the Renderer's 266° and 146°),
  its flow seeded by the token's fingerprint. Two tokens never share a sky.
  It breathes, it follows the finger, and it blooms (`pulse`) when something
  lands. It renders at 0.55× resolution for phones. A CSS gradient replaces
  it without WebGL; it is still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Art within art.** The NFT sits in a conic-gradient iridescent frame that
  tilts with the pointer or the gyroscope, inside the soul. Its memories are
  a constellation inside the Gallery card. Every bundle is an orb with its
  coins in orbit, and every gift is a card with its own generative rings.
- **Five rooms on a dock**: Gallery, Trade, Vault, Social, Studio. Identity
  and the agent lane become a settings sheet and a door, not tabs.
- **One sentence before every signature.** The confirm sheet keeps V1's
  contract: plain words first ("You pay 0.5 ETH and receive about 4.748
  WETH. It locks for 30 days, then frees slowly over 30 days…"), then the
  facts, then one button. A celebration follows the receipt (the soul's
  bloom, particles, a haptic tick, a toast that names what changed).
- **Plain language, progressive depth.** Simple by default ("Lock what I
  receive" is a switch). The facts V1 prints (selectors, calldata,
  fingerprints) are one tap away in the sheet, never removed.

**Budgets.**

| Item | Today | V2 budget |
|---|---|---|
| Shell | 15,000 B gzip | 24,000 B gzip (the shader is ≈ 1.1 KB of it) |
| Panel | 8,192 B gzip | 12,288 B gzip |
| Panel count | `Engine.PANELS = 6` | 8, in an array |

The V1 rules stay:

- no host in `dist/`;
- every write goes through the slab;
- amounts are `BigInt`;
- clocks come from the chain.

## 3. New contracts

All are V1-grade: custom errors, one transient lock per contract, exact
pulls, no admin, CREATE3 salts, a forge suite and a verifier per contract.
Each one names its INVARIANTS rows before it is written.

### 3.1 `Keepsake` — bundles, locks, gifts, slow release, slow selling (supersedes `Locks`)

Keepsake supersedes Locks. A keepsake is an ERC-721 whose token holds up to
8 assets (ETH and ERC-20s, pulled exactly) under one schedule (`start`,
`cliff`, `end`, linear). Its holder claims what has freed. Transferring it
is giving it.

- `wrap(Asset[] assets, Schedule s, address to, bytes32 note) payable returns (uint256 id)`
  - A gift is `to != msg.sender`.
  - `note` is the keccak of a Journal entry written beside it.
- `swapAndWrap(uint256 market, bool baseIn, uint256 amountIn, uint256 minOut, uint64 deadline, Schedule s, address to) payable returns (uint256 id, uint256 out)`
  - Swaps through the Pool with `to = this` and wraps the output: "lock after
    a swap" in one call from a wallet or a Reach.
- `claim(uint256 id)` pays the holder pro rata on every asset. `extend(id, end)` only lengthens.
- **Drip** (slow selling):
  - `setDrip(id, asset, market, floor, maxPerDrip)` is the holder's.
  - `drip(id)` is anyone's. It sells the freed but unclaimed amount of `asset`,
    at most `maxPerDrip`, through the Pool, refusing any execution below
    `floor`, and credits the proceeds to the keepsake.
  - The floor is the only price guard. There is no oracle; the owner names
    the worst price they accept, so a sandwich can cost the drip nothing below
    it.
- `commitmentOf(account)` folds the keepsakes an account holds, so the hub's
  fingerprint keeps covering locked value. Listing a token pins its locks.
- **Art.** Each keepsake's `tokenURI` is an on-chain orb (its assets' hues),
  plus an HTML face showing its contents and the release curve. A gift is a
  website the recipient opens.

### 3.2 `Journal` — memories

- `inscribe(uint256 id, uint8 kind, bytes body, bytes32 ref)`
  - Holder or the token's Reach only, never a renter.
  - Kinds: note, trade, gift, milestone, sealed.
  - `ref` binds a trade (the swap's market, amounts and block) or a keepsake.
- Bodies live in events (≤ 1,024 B plain, ≤ 4,096 B sealed). A sealed body is
  P-256 ciphertext to the author's KeyRegistry key; it is readable by the
  author forever, and by no later holder.
- Storage keeps `count[id]` and a rolling `root[id] = keccak(root, kind,
  keccak(body), ref, author, epoch)`.
- `root` enters the hub's fingerprint. A listing then pins the token's
  memories, and a buyer receives the history they read. The Renderer can draw
  the constellation from `count` and `root` alone.
- During a swap the holder's batch is `[swapAndWrap, inscribe]`. The trade
  facts in `ref` come from the same transaction.

### 3.3 `Bazaar` and the Gallery viewer — any NFT

`Bazaar` is escrow-less listings and offers for any ERC-721 and ERC-1155:

- `list(nft, id, price, currency, expiresAt)`
  - Cancelled by a per-seller nonce.
  - Checked at fill: still the owner, still approved, not expired.
  - Royalty by ERC-2981, capped at 10 %.
  - Proceeds pulled from an `owed` ledger, as in Market.
- `offer` / `accept` use WETH allowances: per token, or across a collection.
- INTACT tokens are routed to `Market`, so their seal and fingerprint
  guarantees stand.

The **Gallery lane** reads any token from the chain alone:

- `tokenURI` and `uri` through `eth_call`.
- `data:` JSON, SVG and HTML decoded in place.
- An HTML `animation_url` runs in a sandboxed `srcdoc` iframe (scripts, no
  same-origin), so it never reaches the wallet.
- `ipfs://` and `ar://` go through a gateway the viewer types once. None is
  shipped, so `dist/` still names no host.
- Provenance comes from `Transfer` logs, journal memories where the
  collection has them, and price history from Bazaar fills.
- The page takes its ambient colours from the art itself, and offers one
  buy button.

### 3.4 `Studio` and `Collection` — launch through NFTs (replaces the coin launchpad)

`Studio.launch(spec)` is called by a token, the creator. Its Reach receives
the proceeds and the ERC-2981 royalty. It deploys a `Collection` (an
ERC-721 clone at a CREATE3 salt) with:

- name, symbol, supply cap, price, window, and an optional referral share
  (`?via=<token>` pays that token's Reach on mint);
- a **site template**: SSTORE2 HTML with typed holes, filled per token (id,
  owner, seed, memory count) by the shared Engine, so `animation_url` is a
  full website served from chain;
- a **room** in Agora that opens with the first mint.

Collections can be launched from any token, including a token of another
collection, which gives lineage (§5).

### 3.5 `Agora` — the social layer (Parley's successor)

- **Identity** is `(collection, id)`; the hub and every Studio collection are
  registered. One inbox, one follow graph, across collections.
- **Rooms:** the commons; a room per collection (its holders speak); a home
  per token; groups; and sealed pairs (Parley's DMs, unchanged in crypto).
- **Reactions:** an optional tip, so ✦ can carry 0.0001 ETH to the author's
  Reach.
- **The feed** interleaves words with Journal memories, Keepsake gifts and
  Bazaar sales by the tokens you follow; each is an event, joined client-side.
- Cooldowns stay keyed by (identity, custody epoch). A sale still silences
  the seller.

## 5. The social layer, and why it compounds

1. **Every token is a doorway.** A Studio token's `animation_url` is a live
   site carrying its collection's room, recent memories and a "mint yours"
   or "buy this". Marketplaces and wallets render `animation_url` HTML, so
   every place that shows the NFT shows the community, without a wallet and
   without us.
2. **Holders bring holders.** A mint link names the token that shared it,
   and that token's Reach is paid a share on chain, in the same transaction.
   Tokens earn by being shown.
3. **Gifts onboard.** A keepsake sent to anyone arrives as a website: the
   note, the coins, the release curve, the giver's room. The gift can carry
   its own claim gas (a slice of its ETH, claimable once), so a newcomer
   needs nothing to start. Sponsored claims grow with EIP-8141 if it ships
   (§6).
4. **Collections are born from tokens.** The creator is a token, not a
   wallet. A collection launched from a token of another collection keeps
   its parent's room linked and can honour the parent's holders with an
   allowlist or discount. Communities form a family tree, and each launch
   strengthens its parent.
5. **One identity, every room.** The same token speaks, follows, reacts,
   gifts and remembers across every collection, so the more collections
   exist, the more each identity is worth.
6. **Memories are provenance.** A storied token is a valuable token. The
   feed shows trades with the journal line beside them ("locked 90 days:
   not selling the top this time"), which is social trading with
   accountability built in.

## 8. Risks

- **The drip is the newest money path.** With no oracle, the floor is the
  whole defence. It gets its own fuzz property: no drip ever executes below
  the floor.
- **HTML in a sandboxed iframe** is how the viewer shows foreign NFTs.
  The sandbox must never include `allow-same-origin`; a Chromium sentence
  holds it.
- **Removing the launchpad changes MVB contracts** (hub, Pool, Catalog). It
  needs the whole battery and a fresh audit of the hub's fingerprint.
- **Bytes.** The redesign is designed to the new ceilings and measured on
  every build. Nothing ships over.
